Seek where each sentence wakes: its capital lies,
six borrowed letters in a false disguise.
I've lines no one waits in, and a tone, but can't speak.
I rhyme. Name me, whom you read, and the key is yours to keep.
Let A be zero, this index is key.
Strip each found letter off of mine, and see.
Should I run short, I'll circle round once more;
then shout aloud the word you came here for.
